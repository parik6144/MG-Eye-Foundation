### **5. Our Services** 

Each service is described according to the nature of the condition, its typical symptoms, and the corresponding treatment approach. 

#### **1. Cataract Surgery** 

Condition: A cataract is a clouding of the natural lens of the eye. 

Common Symptoms: Patients typically report diminished clarity of vision and increased sensitivity to glare, particularly at night. 

Our Approach: The clouded lens is removed and replaced with a new, clear intraocular lens. Most patients return home on the day of surgery. 

#### **2. Glaucoma Treatment** 

Condition: Glaucoma results from elevated pressure within the eye, which can damage the optic nerve over time. 

Common Symptoms: The condition is often asymptomatic in its early stages; peripheral vision may gradually diminish as it progresses. 

Our Approach: Intraocular pressure is measured and monitored. Prescribed drops or laser treatment are used to reduce pressure and preserve vision. 

#### **3. Medical Retina Services** 

Condition: The retina, located at the back of the eye, may leak or swell, a condition frequently associated with diabetes. 

Common Symptoms: Patients may experience blurred, dark, or patchy areas within their field of vision. 

Our Approach: The retina is examined by scan, and injection or laser therapy is administered to protect and preserve vision. 

#### **4. Cornea Treatment** 

Condition: The cornea is the transparent outer surface of the eye. 

Common Symptoms: Symptoms may include pain, redness, or visible damage following injury or infection. 

Our Approach: The cornea is examined using specialised instruments, and the underlying infection or injury is treated to restore clarity. 

#### **5. Dry Eye Clinic** 

Condition: Dry eye occurs when the eye is unable to maintain adequate lubrication. 

Common Symptoms: Patients commonly report burning, gritty, fatigued, or watery eyes. 

Our Approach: The underlying cause is identified, and an appropriate regimen of drops and supportive measures is recommended to relieve symptoms. 

#### **6. Pediatric Eye Care** 

Condition: Children require careful, age-appropriate ophthalmic assessment. 

Common Symptoms: Indicators may include sitting unusually close to a screen, a noticeably weaker eye, or feedback from school regarding a child's vision. 

Our Approach: We assess visual acuity, determine whether corrective lenses are required, and identify any asymmetry in eye strength. 

#### **7. Squint Correction** 

Condition: Squint, or strabismus, occurs when the eyes are not properly aligned. 

Common Symptoms: The eyes may appear to look in different directions; in children, this can result in the weakening of one eye if left untreated. 

Our Approach: Following a thorough assessment, treatment may involve corrective lenses or surgical correction, depending on the case.

### **8. Frequently Asked Questions** 

#### **What are the symptoms of dry eye?** 

Common symptoms include a burning or gritty sensation, redness, light sensitivity, difficulty seeing at night, excessive watering or stinging, and blurred vision. These symptoms may affect one or both eyes. 

#### **What are the early signs of cataract?** 

Please consult our specialists for a full assessment; a member of our clinical team will be pleased to advise you further. 

#### **What eye conditions do you treat?** 

We treat cataract, glaucoma, retinal disease, corneal conditions, dry eye, pediatric eye conditions, squint, and ocular injuries. 

#### **What is the difference between a routine and a medical eye examination?** 

A routine examination assesses general vision and the need for corrective lenses, whereas a medical examination investigates and manages specific eye conditions or diseases. 

#### **Can glaucoma be cured?** 

Glaucoma cannot be cured, but its progression can be effectively managed through appropriate monitoring and treatment. Please speak with our specialists for guidance specific to your condition. 

#### **When should a child be brought in for an eye examination?** 

We recommend an eye examination if a child sits unusually close to screens, exhibits a weaker or misaligned eye, or if concerns are raised by their school regarding their vision. 

#### **Is cataract surgery painful?** 

Cataract surgery is generally well tolerated and performed under local anaesthesia; most patients experience minimal discomfort. 

#### **Do you accept PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat)?** 

Yes, we are empanelled under PM-JAY. Please note that cashless treatment is not currently available.
