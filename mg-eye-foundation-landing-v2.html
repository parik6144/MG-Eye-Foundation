Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to book an appointment? +

No — you can book directly for any service. We'll route you to the right specialist after your initial screening.

How long does cataract surgery recovery take? +

Most patients resume normal activity within 24–48 hours, with full stabilisation over 2–4 weeks.

Do you accept insurance? +

Yes, we work with most major insurers and offer flexible payment plans for uninsured patients.

What should I bring to my first visit? +

A valid ID, any prior prescriptions or scans, and a list of current medications.
